About

I have spent eight years in Schneider Electric's Industrial Automation business. I started by writing firmware, then built and led the connectivity R&D team, and today I run, from the CTO Office, the program behind its next generation of open, real-time industrial networks: OPC UA FX over Time-Sensitive Networking.

The direction I am working toward is a converged, deterministic network that AI agents help configure, diagnose and operate. I sit on the OPC Foundation's Field Level Communication steering committee and its Marketing Control Board, and I started Schneider's Connectivity Community, which now has more than 800 members.

I am French, from Brittany. I studied in Angers and Hong Kong, then worked for Schneider in Boston and Nice; I have been back in Boston since 2021.
